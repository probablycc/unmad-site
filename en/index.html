◆FAQ/05

A few things you might wonder

Still stuck? Check Help & support, or email us.

Q.01What is Unmad?+

A free anger relief app for iPhone. Quick venting and stress relief games for when you’re too mad to think, plus a place to write it all down instead of hitting send.

Q.02Is it free?+

The core features are free, with no ads or subscription.

Q.03Do I need an account?+

No sign-up, no login. Just open it. It works offline too.

Q.04Can my draft get sent to anyone?+

No. Unmad has no send button. When a cool-down ends, you simply get to read your draft again.

Q.05Is there an Android version?+

It’s an iPhone app for now. You can switch between English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese in the app.
