◆Unmad/Games

What’s inside Unmad

10 quick games for letting off steam, 6 ways to get comforted, and a place to write it down

Open Unmad, rate how mad you are, then pick a way out: Let it out, My brain won’t shut up, or Just comfort me. Each game takes a few dozen seconds. Rate it again after and see how far it dropped. Every screenshot here is the actual app.

◆Mode 01/Vent

Let it out

Take it out on something. Hard.

01 / 06

Slice the Noise

One swipe cuts the stuff running through your head in half.

See one that bugs you? Swipe straight through it. The pieces can take another cut. You can also write your own lines, up to five at a time.

02 / 06

Smash This Crap

Write the thing down, then break it one hit at a time.

Tap for a normal hit, or hold and let go for a charged one. On the last hit the whole line blows apart. Then pull down for the Smash Room.

03 / 06

Smash Room

A whole living room. Wreck it.

Tap to hit, hold to hit harder, or grab something from the crate and throw it. Shove the TV, flip the table, sweep the shelf.

04 / 06

Rage Bubbles

Pop a string of bouncing bad feelings.

Pop. Pop. Pop. Don’t think about it.

05 / 06

Bin That Thought

Drag the pop-up in your head into the bin.

Drag it to the bin. No arguing. Thoughts swell up till the bin burps.

06 / 06

Rip the Labels

Every label they slapped on you. Rip it off.

Grab a corner and rip. They’re stuck on all the way round, so spin it and keep going. Or write your own, up to five at a time.

◆Extra/Off the clock

When you’re not playing

+01

Write it down. Don’t send it.

Say it to yourself first and it goes in your log. If it was meant for someone, give it a cool-down (5, 10 or 30 minutes) and decide when it’s up. Unmad has no send button, so nothing ever gets sent for you.

+02

Cool-down log

It doesn’t count how often you got mad. It counts how often you pulled yourself back. Every day you bounce back, an officer goes on duty. No drama after 6 pm? Tap for a sunny stamp. There’s an award at the end of the month. No streaks.

+03

Rage receipts

When a round’s done, put the crap on record: what they did, plus a line if you want. Every month gets its own receipt you can save as an image.

+04

Officer guide

Every officer you’ve met, plus holiday stamps and a few secrets you have to stumble on yourself.

+05

Three quizzes

For when you’re not mad: your anger weather, your fight job, your attachment style. Save the result as an image. Just for fun, not a psych test.
