1.3.0

Coming soon

  • Smash Room is openSmash the thing, pull down, and there’s a whole living room to wreck. Shove the TV, flip the table, sweep the shelf.
  • A train windowFor when you don’t want to hear from anyone, with views from morning to night, spring to winter.
  • Your own linesWrite your own lines to slice or rip, up to five at a time.
  • Bin That ThoughtThoughts swell up till the bin burps.
  • Pick your songDance It Off takes requests now.
  • Rip the LabelsThe blob’s covered all the way round now, so spin it and keep ripping.
  • Longer roundsSlice the Noise and Rage Bubbles rounds now run 40 seconds.
  • SmootherBug fixes and smoother games.

1.2.0

October 2026

  • Two new gamesRip the Labels: every label they slapped on you, ripped off by hand. Squish It: press it, pinch it, and switch between five textures.
  • Slice the NoiseThe pieces can take another cut.
  • Officer guideEvery officer you’ve met, plus holiday stamps and a few secrets.

  • A few secretsNot saying where.

1.1.0

October 2026

  • Rage receiptsWhen a round’s done, tap “Receipt?” and put the crap on record: what they did, plus a line if you want. It goes on that day in your log, and every month gets its own receipt you can save as an image.
  • A new logEvery day you bounce back, an officer goes on duty in your log, picked by what got you there. Finish a Let it out or My brain won’t shut up game and you get their stamp.
  • New facesThe anger slider has a face now, you get a soft landing once you’re under 30, and the Dance captain moves to the beat of each song.
  • SturdierHalf-written notes stay put, and your number is saved the moment you report it. If something ever breaks, you get a button back home instead of the app closing on you.
